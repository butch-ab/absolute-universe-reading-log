How this log works

Pick a series or follow publication order. Read the issue. Log status, a rating, and your thoughts here. Then tell Grok you finished it — that is when critic reviews get written into this file. Until then the review panel stays locked, even if you already marked the issue Read.

House rule

No scores, quotes, or recaps from AIPT, Multiversity, Comic Book Roundup, or anyone else until you report a Read. First impressions stay yours. After that, reviews are spoiler-light: tone and craft, not last-page surprises.

Start-here shortcuts

Start at Superman (2023) #1, Wonder Woman (2023) #1, Flash (2023) #1, Green Lantern (2023) #1, or the 2025 Batman #1. All In Special frames the era. Next Level #1s in 2026 are standalone-friendly. Action, Detective, Nightwing, and other old-number books start at their All In jump-on issue, not at their original #1.

Your data
